## MPEG-1 Audio (First attempt at compressing music)
*Sampling frequencies*: 32 kHz, 44.1 kHz, 49 kHz
*Precisions*: 16 bits
*Three compression layers*
- Layer 1: 32-448kb/s, target 192 kb/s
- Layer 2: 32-384 kb/s, target 128 kb/s
- Layer 3: 32-320 kb/s, target 64 kb/s

# MPEG-2 Advanced Audio Coding (AAC)
At this stage, compression techniques tries to target a higher quality medium (cinema, music, ...)
- Supports mono/stereo and multichannel audio (up to 5.1 in MPEG-2 AAC).
- Transform-based perceptual coding: MDCT, non-uniform quantization, Huffman coding and rate–distortion control.
- Compared with **MP3**: higher frequency resolution (1024 lines), improved joint stereo and block switching.
- Additional tools include temporal noise shaping, prediction and channel coupling

![[5-Audio-coding-standards.pdf#page=12&rect=34,76,949,364|5-Audio-coding-standards, p.12]]
	MDCT is not done in 32 different bands
	Quantization is more sofisticated

MP3 codes frames individually, while AAC does take some temporal information for prediction purposes (steady sounds, ...)

The most important change is that AAC tackles sound as **sound objects**
- Instead of tracks, we now treat the objects that make the sounds

AAC was then greatly improved with MPEG4
