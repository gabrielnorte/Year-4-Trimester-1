
Mobility type circuits 

Taking the impedance as $Z = \frac{V}{I}$:
- $V$ as pressure and $I$ as current - IMPEDANCE TYPE CIRCUIT
- $V$ as current and $V$ as pressure - MOBILITY TYPE CIRCUIT

They're just the same circuit but explained differently
