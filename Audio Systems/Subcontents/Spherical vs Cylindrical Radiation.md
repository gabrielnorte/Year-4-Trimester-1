**Stacking loudspeakers vertically, like in outside concerts, sound emission becomes cylindrical**
- Reduces volume losses due to distance
![[AES_T1_Sound_and_Acoustics_26-27.pdf#page=7&rect=17,13,709,450|AES_T1_Sound_and_Acoustics_26-27, p.7]]

Spherical radiation
- Double the distance, -6 dB
- Double the sound pressure (SPL), +6dB

Cylindrical radiation
- Double the distance, -3 dB
- Double the sound pressure (SPL), +3dB
